## Enrollment, mining and accounting

Each wallet announcement contains 512 original signed transfers. Announce six batches/s for 1,800 seconds, distributing batch positions across four peer sockets round-robin. Each wallet serves full transactions after receiving stock SV's native `getdata`. In R preserve both the batch's arrival position and its original source ordinal. Give every arm the same 300 work opportunities at six-second intervals. The first three of each four go to a real Scary Stratum worker, the fourth to stock SV. Keep the stock submission and worker deadlines, readbacks, ordinary-work policy, two-successor settlement rule and up to 300 seconds' drain of the inventory control. Reannounce only residual missing payments after t=1,818 s at no more than 3,072/s. All original slots, socket failures, unready options, unsuccessful solves and detached blocks count. A slot unresolved at the next deadline makes that next slot missed.

For each selected block, keep its full wire, native arrival observations, three independent stock body digests, entrant validation and independent active-chain readbacks. Attribute Scary origin by matching its Stratum solution header and issued parent to those bytes. Retain the native transaction-intake counters, batch identities, request and served bitmaps, and script and source-parent checks. A sequence of per-node samples establishes a source route; it cannot establish individual relay provenance beyond the fields actually recorded. Classify each included input as spending a same-block parent, an immediately preceding block, or an older observed block. A validated in-template parent can supply a child without the confirmed-output successor requirement.

The regular-origin criterion requires at least one independently selected, nonempty Scary block for an opportunity in **each** of the six five-minute primary intervals. Slot indices 0–49 belong to interval one, 50–99 to interval two, and so on. The joint target additionally requires all 5,529,600 distinct ordinary payments confirmed, two selected successors, every source cohort reconciled, all transaction scripts checked by the entrant, zero foreign or duplicate payments, and at least 3,000 confirmed payments/s over the complete interval through final readback. Report each criterion separately. Report accepted Scary payment counts by slot interval, Scary payment throughput over that same complete interval, observed and missing work slots, and system-wide throughput. Preserve the same elapsed denominator if the entrant produces zero blocks. No assumption about average mainnet block cadence or orphan risk follows from these regtest intervals.

Sample the existing resource gauges about once per second and stock queue/mempool/peer activity about every five seconds. Preserve at least 20 GiB of host capacity and 12 GiB in the Docker filesystem. Record source and terminal free capacity, sample minima and any reserve stop. Hold the workstation's measurement lock through the run. Check available *allocated* storage and projected peak usage before setup. Freeze earlier cohorts after terminal observations. Preserve their independently restorable, checksum-verified evidence and source, separating compact chain replay from any full service-state archive. Do not enroll a replacement timed session for a missed target. Report incomplete enrollment or interrupted results explicitly.

A future changed-core cold run requires a separate registration that pins its executable and preserves the original cold offer order.
