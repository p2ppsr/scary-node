It retrieved and validated both bodies while the reference still had no successor, 1,529 ms and 1,904 ms after each send. Sixteen fresh sizing sessions scheduled 512 tip races across the witnessed/full and full/full arms. In the witnessed/full arm, Scary Node was ready on 226 of 256 opportunities and the full miner on 256. Scary Node recorded 68 stale discoveries out of 145 (46.90%), and the full miner recorded 12 out of 191 (6.28%). The paired difference was +40.61 percentage points, with a session-clustered 95% interval of +28.72 to +52.51. The full/full control differed by +0.43 points (−12.77 to +13.64). The pilot serves to size the fresh evaluation. It called for **2,263 independent sessions, or 72,416 scheduled opportunities**, to target a one-percentage-point half-width. [Sizing summary](evidence/chronicle-native-sizing.json) · [Raw scheduled opportunities](evidence/chronicle-native-pilot)

Every sole discovery settled. Scary Node won 12 of 80 ties against the full miner. The full/full lanes split 85 ties 44 to 41. Among sole discoveries, the first observed active hash on either receiver took a median of 590 ms for Scary Node and 126 ms for the full miner. Both full/full lanes took about 125 ms. Stock SV Node and Scary Node used local native links, but the measured propagation times differed. These outcomes describe the engineering build's publication behavior and do not establish a stale-rate difference under equal end-to-end propagation. [Sole discoveries, ties and latency](evidence/chronicle-native-latency.json)

The original evaluation closed before its registered endpoint. [Run the comparison](production-cohort.md)
